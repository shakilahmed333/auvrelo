Care Guide

Leather is made to be used. A little care keeps it looking good for years, and the marks of wear are part of what makes each piece yours.

Leather

  • Wipe clean with a soft, slightly damp cloth, then let it air-dry away from direct sun and heat.
  • Condition every few months with a leather conditioner, testing it first on a hidden spot.
  • If it gets wet, blot (don't rub) and let it dry naturally. Never use a hairdryer or heater.
  • Scuffs and patina are normal and often soften with conditioning and time.

Storage

  • Store bags stuffed with paper so they hold their shape, in a breathable dust bag rather than plastic.
  • Keep them in a cool, dry place out of direct sunlight.

Brass and metal hardware

Wipe with a dry cloth. Brass naturally darkens over time; a brass polish will brighten it if you prefer.

Care instructions for each product are also listed on its product page.
