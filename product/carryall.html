Leather & care

Every AUVRELO piece is built by hand, judged on how it holds up in year three rather than how it photographs on day one. Wipe clean with a soft, slightly damp cloth, then let it air-dry away from direct sun and heat. Condition every few months with a leather conditioner, testing it first on a hidden spot. If it gets wet, blot (don't rub) and let it dry naturally. Never use a hairdryer or heater. Store bags stuffed with paper so they hold their shape, in a breathable dust bag rather than plastic.
